A masterpiece of latin music
Clube da Esquina is the 5th studio album by Milton Nascimento and the debut studio album by Lô Borges, both who are Brazilian MPB musicians, for those that don’t know, MPB means musica popular brasileira. This is the first in a series that was created of the same name. This album was in secret in protest to the government of Brazil at the time which like almost the rest of South America, were right-wing military dictatorships that suppressed, tortured and murdered tens of thousands of left-wing political opponents and those who criticise the regime. They got away with it though by having no mention of the regime and instead having a pro love and unity message in the album, essentially the point was to give the Brazilian people feelings and senses of love and unity in a period that was marked by violence and authoritarianism. The album was not well received by critics at the time but was a commercial success and is now re-evaluated and regarded as the greatest Brazilian album ever made and one of the most recognizable albums in Brazil by many Brazilians and often compared to releases such as Sgt. Pepper’s and Pet Sounds.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - TUDO QUE VOCÊ PODIA SER is one of my favorite songs off the album and the best song done by Milton Nascimento. The song opens with some plucky guitar strums and some singing from Milton, all the songs are obviously in Portuguese but with a bit of some Spanish in some songs too. The song features some progressive and building structures with some psychedelic sounds and rhythms throughout as well.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - CAIS is another one of my favorite songs off the album. This song features some slower rhythms and sounds with a steady but slow melody with some strings and some soft guitar strums. The song then goes into some piano melodies and some humming from Milton on top of it that leads on until the song fades out.

LÔ BORGES - O TREM AZUL is one of my favorite songs off the album. This song features a slow and mellow sound with some soft strumming and cymbal taps with a beautiful and tropical sound to it. The singing from Borges is very angelic and the backing vocals really add a lot of atmosphere. The song is a bit long running over 4 minutes but its buildup with a mellow lead really does a lot to keep me interested.

MILTON NASCIMENTO & BETO GUEDES - SAÍDAS E BANDEIRAS N° 1 is a good song but one of the weakest off the album but still decent, it features another MPB musician named Beto Guedes on it and it is also only 45 seconds long. The song features some jumpy and plucky melodies with some psychedelic rhythms throughout and ends with an abrupt fade out.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - NUVEM CIGANA is one of my favorite songs off the album. The song opens with some soft strums and features some angelic string arrangements with some horn arrangements heard on the song as well making it feel almost cinematic at times in a strange way but also a very fun and beautiful tone to it.

MILTON NASCIMENTO & LÔ BORGES - CRAVE E CANELA is another one of my favorite songs off the album and features both musicians on it. This song features a tropical sound with some whistles and plucky strums throughout. The rhythms are a bit bouncy and tense but with a colorful and uplifting sound to it as well.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - DOS CRUCES is another one of my favorite songs off the album. It is the album's longest song running almost 5 and a half minutes. The song features some slow melodies in the beginning with soft plucks but it also features some sharper strums and steady build up for the direction the song picks up into later with some slapping rhythms and clicks and clacks as the music picks up with some improvisation taking place as well and guitars building up with Milton’s vocals blasting through as he bellows and some backing vocals come in with some noisy guitars riffs making way too.

LÔ BORGES - UM GIRASSOL DA COR DE SEU CABELO is my favorite song off the album. The song has an emotional atmosphere to it starting slow with some pianos and soft singing from Borges and some improvisation heard throughout too. The song features some steady progressions and tasteful build ups. The song also feature some string arrangements and soon the song goes into an eerie interlude with some strings and sharp arrangements creating a darker and noisy sound until some pianos come in that build up into an absolutely gorgeous musical explosion that takes fold with Borges vocals shouted through bouncy drumming and some reverbed backing vocals and fast and plucky strums making possibly the best moment in Brazilian musical history.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - SAN VICENTE is a good song. This song features a much more peaceful and atmospheric sound with some soft singing and guitar strums and plucks with a psychedelic and airy feel to it. The song also features some faster and a bit more chaotic rhythms as it progresses though with tones of sounds like chimes, dings and other notes passing through the speaker.

LÔ BORGES - ESTRELAS is a good song but a very short one lasting only 28 seconds making it one of the albums weakest songs. The song features some angelic backing vocals and soft plucks with a dreamy sound and some soft singing from Borges before ending abruptly.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - CLUBE DA ESQUINA N° 2 is one of my favorite songs off the album. It’s an instrumental song that features some tropical plucks and a rhythmic and mellow but still busy sound with some strings and other beautiful arrangements. The song features some humming from Milton heard on it but even though it's instrumental, the arrangements and other beautiful portions heard throughout the song really make it stand out and one of the albums best.

LÔ BORGES - PAISAGEM DA JANELA is another one of my favorite songs off the album. This song features a peaceful and highly tropical sound with some reverbed guitars that sound slippery and bright with some colorful and psychedelic bumpy melodies with an unbelievably happy sound that you can’t just help but smile at. The song features some bright piano chords heard throughout it as well that I really liked a lot too.

MILTON NASCIMENTO & ALAÍDE COSTA - ME DEIXA EM PAZ is a good song, the musician it features is a Samba musician. This song opens with some soft strums and features Costa’s sung vocals on it and some humming from Milton with her singing. The song also features some strummed guitars and some mixes of bossa nova and samba within the song which I thought was an interesting inclusion within, the song also builds up with some faster rhythms and clunky portions that I enjoyed as well.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - OS POVOS is a good song. It features some slow melodies and some mellow portions. It’s one of the album's longest songs, running 4 and a half minutes. It does take a bit to build though and not a lot really changes in it but it's relaxing, psychedelic and very atmospheric as well which helps drive it.

MILTON NASCIMENTO & BETO GUEDES - SAÍDAS E BANDEIRAS N° 2 is a good song but one of the albums weakest songs for me. It features some tropical sounds with some bright string arrangements and other bouncy portions heard throughout. I think a problem with it though is it does feel slightly incomplete like it could have gone on longer, it's only a minute and a half long but it's still a decent song.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - UM GOSTA DEL SOL is a good song. The song opens with some peaceful piano melodies and some soft singing from Milton as well. It is one of the longer songs off the album running for almost 4 and a half minutes. The singing from Milton sounds reverbed with some other portions that lead to some build up but stays at an incredibly steady pace at the beginning but the build up with these cinematic string arrangements come in with some pianos over it that finishes it off.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - PELO AMOR DE DEUS is a good song but one of the weakest off the album for me. The song features some sharper and noisier sounds and a bit of some experimental portions with high pitched synthesizers and also some noisier guitars with some layered up feedback throughout and some swirling chaotic moments too.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - LILIA is a good song. It is an instrumental song and features some plucky strums and bouncy guitars with some humming from Milton featured on it and also some portions that go on throughout the song.

LÔ BORGES - TREM DE DOIDO is one of my favorite songs off the album. This song features a feedback heavy guitar with some soft singing from Borges. The song almost feels like shoegaze at times with how rich the feedback is. The song features some other pluckier sounds hidden within as well and some angelic sounding backing vocals heard throughout the song as well.

MILTON NASCIMENTO & BETO GUEDES - NADA SERÁ COMO ANTES is another one of my favorite songs off the album. This is the final song to feature Beto Guedes on it and it features a happy and uplifting sound with some jumpy and rhythmic guitar strums with some peaceful flutes heard throughout the song as well. The song also features some keyboards and a bit of a tumbling sound near the end as well.

MILTON NASCIMENTO - AO QUE VAI NASCER is my least favorite song off the album but it's still a good song. The song features a bit of a more uneasy sound with some steady rising guitars and some strummed portions with some soft singing from Milton heard on it as well. I think the song features a bit of a repetitive rhythm which is why it was my least favorite but I still think it’s colorful and still an enjoyable listen.

Overall, I thought Clube da esquina was an excellent album, the masterpiece of both musicians and the greatest album to ever come out of Latin America in my opinion. While the lyrics may be in Portuguese, you still feel the emotion, every nerve and feeling felt from the musicians, it's incredibly diverse and takes beautiful mixtures and combinations of several latin styles of music. There's a good reason why it's hailed as a masterpiece today and will continue to influence artists with its legacy.
