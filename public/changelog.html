Version 2.7.0

September 2026

  • Improved

    Update notes open automatically

    See what has changed whenever EvenBetterRYM is updated.

  • New

    Navigate vote histories more easily

    Switch directly between music genre, film genre, and descriptor vote histories while keeping the selected user.

  • New

    Control vote statistics

    Choose whether vote counts and percentages are shown on genre and descriptor voting pages.

  • New

    Clear release and recording dates

    Use the new Clear buttons when submitting or editing a release to remove release or recording dates quickly.

  • New

    Fine-tune image crops

    Adjust each crop edge by 10 or 50 pixels from the image crop editor. Thanks to AnotherBubblebath for the first version of the implementation.

  • Improved

    Smoother visibility controls

    Hidden ratings and reviews no longer flash during page loading, and ratings appear immediately after you rate a release.
